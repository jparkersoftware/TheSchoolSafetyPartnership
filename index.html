You're responsible for health and safety. You don't have to carry it alone.

Heads, business managers and governors hold real duties for the safety of pupils, staff and visitors, on top of everything else a school asks of them.

We give you clear, practical advice from someone who knows how schools actually run, so you can make good decisions, fix what matters first, and get back to the work of educating children.

Every school must appoint a competent person to help it meet its health and safety duties. We can be that support for your school.

How we help

Four ways we support schools. Use one on its own, or combine them for a complete picture.

Health and safety consultancy

Practical, tailored advice that helps your school meet its legal duties and keep pupils, staff and visitors safe.

Good for: schools that want a trusted adviser on hand.

What's included
  • Health and safety audits to identify risks and areas for improvement
  • Policies and procedures written for your school
  • Risk assessments and guidance on effective control measures
  • Compliance support on current legislation and good practice
  • Staff training that builds confidence and a positive safety culture
  • Ongoing advice when new concerns or questions come up

Audits and compliance

A clear, practical review of your current arrangements, so you know exactly where you stand.

Good for: a new head or business manager, or independent schools preparing for inspection.

What's included
  • Risks identified across your premises, activities and working practices
  • A review of your existing arrangements, policies and procedures
  • Gaps and areas of concern highlighted plainly
  • Practical improvements that suit your school's needs and resources
  • Actions prioritised so you tackle the most important issues first

Action planning

Clear, prioritised recommendations that turn findings into a practical roadmap your team can follow.

Good for: schools with a list of issues and no clear order to tackle them.

What's included
  • Next steps set out in a clear, manageable plan
  • Recommendations prioritised by risk, urgency and impact
  • Quick wins alongside longer-term improvements
  • Clear responsibilities, so staff know what's expected of them
  • Support for confident decisions and ongoing compliance

Project management

Support to plan, organise and coordinate building and site projects, safely and with minimum disruption.

Good for: summer works, refurbishments and contractor-heavy projects.

What's included
  • Clear objectives and an agreed project scope
  • A realistic plan with key milestones and timescales
  • Responsibilities agreed, so everyone understands their role
  • Coordination of contractors, suppliers and stakeholders
  • Progress, risks and actions monitored throughout

How it works

A straightforward process, built around the way schools run.

  1. A free call

    Thirty minutes to talk about your school, your concerns and what you need.

  2. A site visit

    We walk your site, review your documents and talk to the people who run it day to day.

  3. Your report and action plan

    A plain-English report, with actions ranked by risk so you know what to do first.

  4. Support to put it right

    Help to put the plan into practice, and advice whenever new questions come up.

Hello, I'm Clare

I've spent 15 years in school leadership, most of it in specialist schools for pupils with special educational needs, working across health and safety, safeguarding and school business management. Most recently I've led compliance across a group of independent specialist schools, developing and running the systems that keep several sites safe and compliant.

My career began in construction services, which gives me a practical eye for building work, contractors and site safety. I also volunteer on the local advisory board of a Cambridgeshire primary school, as its health and safety lead.

I know health and safety has to fit alongside teaching, budgets and everything else on your plate. So my advice is straightforward and proportionate, and focused on what makes a genuine difference to pupils, staff and visitors.

Does our school have to have a competent person for health and safety?

Yes. Every school must appoint a competent person to help it meet its health and safety duties. That help can come from someone on your staff, your local authority or trust, or an independent adviser like us.

We already buy in health and safety support. Can you still help?

Yes. Some schools ask us for an independent audit or a second opinion, or for help with a particular project such as summer building works.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your school and what you need. After a free call, we'll send you a clear, fixed quote before any work starts.

Which areas do you cover?

What kinds of schools do you work with?

Primary and secondary schools, academies and trusts, and independent schools, including specialist schools for pupils with special educational needs.
