How I can help

Four ways I work with schools. Use one on its own, or combine them.

Health and safety consultancy

Advice tailored to your school, so you meet your legal duties and keep pupils, staff and visitors safe.

Good for: schools that want an adviser on hand all year.

What's included
  • Your health and safety policy and procedures, written for your school
  • Risk assessments for your site, activities and trips, and how to control the risks
  • Help keeping up with changes in legislation and guidance
  • Staff training that builds confidence and a positive safety culture
  • Someone to call when a new concern or question comes up

Audits and compliance

A full review of your current arrangements, so you know exactly where you stand.

Good for: a new head or business manager, or independent and special schools preparing for inspection against the Independent School Standards.

What's included
  • A walk of your whole site: buildings, plant rooms, grounds and outdoor areas
  • A check of your key records, such as your fire risk assessment, asbestos register, water hygiene records and inspection certificates
  • A review of your policy, risk assessments and procedures
  • Gaps and concerns set out plainly, the most serious first
  • Improvements that suit your school's budget and resources

Action planning

Turns findings into a plan your team can follow, with the most important actions first.

Good for: schools with a list of issues and no clear order to tackle them.

What's included
  • Every action ranked by risk, urgency and impact
  • An owner and a target date for each action
  • Quick wins alongside longer-term improvements
  • A summary governors can use to track progress

Project management

Help to plan and coordinate building and site projects, safely and with as little disruption as possible.

Good for: summer works, refurbishments and contractor-heavy projects.

What's included
  • An agreed scope and a realistic plan with milestones
  • Contractor checks: insurance, risk assessments and method statements, and permits to work
  • Work areas kept apart from pupils, staff and visitors
  • Coordination of contractors, suppliers and school staff
  • Progress, risks and actions monitored through to handover

  1. A free call

Does our school have to have a competent person for health and safety?

Yes. Health and safety law requires every employer, including whoever employs your school's staff, to appoint one or more competent people to help meet its duties (Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, regulation 7). That help can come from someone on your staff, your local authority or trust, or an independent adviser like me.

We already buy in health and safety support. Can you still help?

Yes. Some schools ask me for an independent audit or a second opinion, or for help with a particular project such as summer building works.
