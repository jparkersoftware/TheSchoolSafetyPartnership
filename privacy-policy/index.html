Privacy Notice

The School Safety Partnership Ltd · Version 1.2, November 2026

The School Safety Partnership Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a health and safety consultancy providing health, safety and compliance support to schools. We are the data controller for the personal information described in this notice. We are registered in England and Wales (company number 17482599) and with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO registration number ZC283335).

1. Who this notice applies to

  • Clients and prospective clients, including school leaders, business managers and other staff who instruct us or enquire about our services.
  • Staff and visitors at client schools, where our work involves them on site or touches their health, safety or welfare.
  • Suppliers and associates, including contractors and other professionals we work with.
  • Website visitors and enquirers who contact us by email, phone or through this website.

2. What personal information we collect

We only collect what we need for the purpose in front of us. Depending on how you deal with us, this may include:

  • Contact details: name, job title, organisation, work email address and telephone number.
  • Correspondence: emails, letters, notes of calls and meetings, and documents you send us.
  • Contract and service information: instructions, scope of work, site details, and invoicing and payment records.
  • Health and safety information where relevant to a commission, such as accidents, incidents, near misses, risk assessments, and the health or welfare of named individuals.
  • Website information: your IP address and the pages you request, which our website host records automatically (see section 10).

We collect special category data (for example, health information) only where it is genuinely necessary for the health and safety purpose, and we handle it with additional care.

3. How we collect it

  • Directly from you.
  • From your school or organisation, for example when a colleague gives us your details as a contact.
  • In the course of our work, such as site visits, audits, investigations, meetings and training.
  • Automatically when you visit this website.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for anyone else’s marketing. We share it only with:

  • Suppliers and associates such as self-employed associates, IT providers, accountants and insurers, under contract.
  • Client organisations, where a commission requires us to report to the school that instructed us.
  • Professional advisers and regulators, such as legal advisers, insurers and the Health and Safety Executive, where the law requires it or it is necessary to protect someone’s safety.
  • Service providers that host or run our systems, namely our website host, our email provider and FormSubmit, the service that delivers messages from our website enquiry form to our inbox.

Where we use a service provider to handle personal information for us, we have a written agreement that requires them to protect it and act only on our instructions.

6. International transfers

We do not routinely transfer personal information outside the UK. Our website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. in the United States, which receives the technical information described in section 10, and our enquiry form is delivered by FormSubmit, which may also process information outside the UK. If any provider stores personal information outside the UK, we check that appropriate safeguards are in place first, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses, or an adequacy regulation.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to work: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client records and project files: for the engagement period plus 6 years.
  • Health and safety records: in line with retention expectations for the school and health and safety legislation.
  • Financial and tax records: 6 years from the end of the relevant financial year.

At the end of the period, we delete, securely destroy or anonymise the information.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including access controls, encrypted devices and secure storage. Access is on a need-to-know basis, and anyone working with us receives data protection guidance.

9. Your rights

Under data protection law you have the right to:

  • be informed about how we use your information;
  • access the information we hold about you (a subject access request);
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have information erased where we have no good reason to keep it;
  • restrict or object to our use of your information, including objecting to marketing at any time;
  • receive your information in a portable format; and
  • withdraw consent, where we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 11. We will respond within one month, we may need to confirm your identity, and there is no charge. If you have a concern, please raise it with us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

10. This website, cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics tools, so there is no cookie banner. Our website host automatically records technical information such as your IP address and the pages you request, which we do not use to identify you. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads. If we add analytics or cookies in future, we will update this notice and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

If you use the enquiry form, the details you enter (name, school or trust, role, email address, telephone number if given, and your message) are passed through FormSubmit, an email-forwarding service, and delivered to us by email. We use them only to reply to your enquiry. FormSubmit may process this information outside the UK.
